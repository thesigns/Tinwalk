Tinwalk

Turn your walks into a survival game.

  • Create a shelter at home.
  • Walk out into the wasteland and search areas for supplies. Each search needs a spot at least 200 m away from your shelter and earlier searches.
  • Bring your loot and any survivors you meet back to the shelter.

Tinwalk uses your location while the game is open. Your game is saved only on this device.

Tinwalk needs your location

The game is played on real walks, so it can't work without access to your location.

Allow location access for this site in your browser settings, then tap Try again.
